Selected work

  • Argos Research · 2026

    Off-road autonomy

    Integrated terrain perception, sensor fusion, planning, and vehicle control in a model-based driving stack. Used replay to trace how errors in terrain estimates affected the paths the planner selected.

  • Environment and Climate Change Canada · 2025

    Instrument data quality and alignment

    Aligned greenhouse gas instrument logs with GPS-timed survey data using peak matching and correlation. Recovered missing readings from the aligned logs and automated Python quality checks and reports.

  • Argos Research · 2026

    Browser agents and structured web extraction

    Built the runtime that launches parallel browser agents, controls access to Playwright-backed and custom tools, and caps tool calls. Added context pruning and stop conditions for stalled agents. Built parts of the pipeline that deduplicates page content and extracts structured records.

  • Argos Research · 2026

    Learning to drive from images

    Contributed to teacher–student training in Isaac Lab and helped adapt it to BeamNG. The experiments tested whether a policy using camera images could learn driving behavior from a teacher with access to simulator state.

  • Environment and Climate Change Canada · 2023

    Methane emissions estimation

    Ran and automated statistical inversion simulations to estimate landfill methane emissions. Compared model fits across weather inputs and analyzed estimates from individual survey passes. Co-authored a peer-reviewed paper on methane emissions.

  • Argos Research · 2026

    Image feature search

    Built a benchmark to compare search methods for small programs that extract image features. Evaluated feature transfer across datasets using linear models and random-feature baselines.

About

I’m most excited by machine learning research and building models end to end, from designing experiments and preparing data to training models and figuring out where they fail. I’m especially interested in robotics and the energy sector, where I’d like to build and test new systems that solve physical problems.

I studied physics and computing at the University of Waterloo. I worked on autonomy and machine learning at Argos Research and on atmospheric emissions and instrument data at Environment and Climate Change Canada.
