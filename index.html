Selected work

Argos Research · 2026

  • Robotics

    Off-road autonomy

    Integrated terrain perception, sensor fusion, planning, and vehicle control in a model-based driving stack. Used replay to trace how errors in terrain estimates affected the paths the planner selected.

  • Machine learning

    Learning to drive from images

    Contributed to teacher–student training in Isaac Lab and helped adapt it to BeamNG. The experiments tested whether a policy using camera images could learn driving behavior from a teacher with access to simulator state.

  • Computer vision

    Image feature search

    Built a benchmark to compare search methods for small programs that extract image features. Evaluated whether the features remained useful when applied to new datasets.

  • Data systems

    Structured web extraction

    Built the runtime that launches parallel browser agents, controls access to Playwright-backed and custom tools, and caps tool calls. Added context pruning and stop conditions for stalled agents. Also built parts of the pipeline that turns collected pages into structured records.

About

My background is in physics and computing at the University of Waterloo. At Argos Research, I worked across robotics simulation, machine learning, and software systems as a Member of Technical Staff.

At Environment and Climate Change Canada, I ran and analyzed atmospheric emissions simulations and worked on aligning and checking instrument data.
